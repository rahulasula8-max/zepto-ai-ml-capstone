Zepto Gift Cards:
Zepto Digital Gift Cards are available for purchase in denominations ranging from ₹250 to ₹10,000. Gift cards are delivered electronically via email or SMS with a unique 16-digit card code and a 6-digit security PIN. Zepto Gift Cards are valid for exactly 12 months from the date of issuance and cannot be extended or reloaded. To redeem a gift card, enter the code and PIN at the checkout payment screen under 'Add Gift Card / Voucher'. Any remaining balance will stay attached to the gift card code for subsequent purchases. Gift cards are non-refundable and cannot be redeemed for physical cash.
