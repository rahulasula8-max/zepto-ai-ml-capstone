Zepto Damaged or Missing Items Policy:
If any item in your order arrives damaged, leaking, expired, or is missing from your package, please report the issue within 24 hours of delivery via the 'Help & Support' section of the Zepto application. Customers should upload a clear photograph of the damaged item showing the product label, packaging condition, and batch number where applicable. For missing items, our dark store dispatch team conducts an immediate CCTV packaging audit. Once validated by customer support, an instant replacement delivery is dispatched within 30 minutes, or a full refund is credited to Zepto Cash or original payment mode.
