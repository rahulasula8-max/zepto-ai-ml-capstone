Zepto Returns & Refunds Policy:
Customers may request returns or refunds for eligible items directly via the Zepto mobile app under the Order History section. For perishable items including fresh fruits, vegetables, dairy, and bakery products, return or refund requests must be initiated within 2 hours of delivery. For non-perishable packaged consumer goods, household items, and personal care products, requests can be filed within 24 hours of delivery. Refunds approved for damaged, spoiled, or missing items are credited instantly to Zepto Cash wallet within 2 hours, or processed back to the original payment source (credit/debit card, UPI, net banking) within 5 to 7 business days depending on the customer's bank.
