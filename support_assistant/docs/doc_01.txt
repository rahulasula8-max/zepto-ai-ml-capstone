Zepto Delivery Policy:
Zepto offers ultra-fast grocery delivery across designated serviceable pin codes. Standard delivery operates within 10 to 15 minutes of order confirmation during normal operational hours (6:00 AM to 1:00 AM IST). In situations of adverse weather, peak order surge, or road restrictions, estimated delivery times may adjust to 25-45 minutes. A nominal delivery fee of ₹15 to ₹35 applies to orders below ₹199; orders above ₹199 qualify for free standard delivery. Contactless delivery is enabled by default for all online prepaid orders. Delivery partners will place the order at your doorstep and notify you via the app. For orders containing age-restricted or high-value items, recipient physical verification may be required upon handover.
