Zepto Order Cancellation Policy:
Customers can cancel an order free of charge within 60 seconds of order confirmation directly through the app interface. Once 60 seconds have elapsed or after the dark store warehouse has begun packing the items, cancellation is disabled to maintain rapid 10-minute turnaround and prevent perishable food waste. If an order cannot be fulfilled by Zepto due to operational disruption or out-of-stock items, the order will be cancelled automatically by our system with a 100% full refund credited immediately to the original payment mode along with an apology discount voucher.
