Zepto Order Tracking:
Once an order is successfully placed, customers can track its progress in real time via the live map view in the Zepto app. The tracking interface provides dynamic milestone updates: 'Order Received', 'Packing at Dark Store', 'Dispatched with Delivery Partner', and 'Arriving at Doorstep'. Real-time GPS location of the assigned delivery partner and dynamic Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA) in minutes are displayed continuously. Customers can contact the delivery partner directly via masked in-app phone calling once dispatch is confirmed. Push notifications are automatically dispatched when the partner is within 500 meters of the delivery destination.
