Zepto Membership Tiers (Zepto Pass):
Zepto Pass is our premium subscription service designed to offer maximum savings for frequent shoppers. Zepto Pass is available in two plans: Monthly Pass at ₹99/month and Annual Pass at ₹899/year. Members enjoy unlimited free deliveries on all grocery and essentials orders exceeding ₹99. In addition, Zepto Pass members receive up to 20% extra discount on select partner brand categories, priority customer support routing, and exclusive early access to flash sales and seasonal promotions. Membership auto-renews at the end of each billing cycle unless cancelled at least 24 hours prior to renewal via app account settings.
